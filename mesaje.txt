feat: reactive store, known services and learning mode

- Guard.Subscribe / Guard.GetSnapshot: observable store with immutable
  snapshots (Running, Bot, LastFinding, Statistics), compatible with
  useSyncExternalStore, Vue refs, Angular signals and Svelte stores.
- Challenge proof state changes are published in the Bot event/snapshot.
- Services: catalog of well-known third parties that extends allowed
  origins; Sentry and zone.js wrappers are trusted by following their
  markers back to the legitimate function (declared services only).
- Learning mode: records non-allowed third-party origins and proposes
  them via GetLearnedConfig().
- Docs (EN/ES), unit and e2e tests, changeset.
